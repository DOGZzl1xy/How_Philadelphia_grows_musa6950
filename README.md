# Final Project

**English** | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

MUSA 6950 AI for Urban Sustainability, final project

**Teaching Annual Aerial Imagery to See Growth: Tracking Philadelphia's Gentle Densification with LiDAR-Supervised Foundation Models**

**Storyline.** Philadelphia grows quietly, one lot at a time, but the city does not know where or when. LiDAR measures height but is flown only every three to four years; aerial photographs come every year but show no height. This project lets LiDAR teach the photographs to "see height". On the sealed test (8 ZIP codes, 2022→2025, run once), new-construction counts correlate with LiDAR at 0.96 per 250 m cell, so the model can fill the years without LiDAR. Added storeys reach only 0.42: an extra storey cannot be seen from directly above, so their counts come from LiDAR years only; street-level photographs read by a vision-language model then find them house by house (1,538 marked, about four in five right when checked by hand) without counting them. With new construction dated year by year, the project then asks where, how much and when the city densified (RQ2), and whether permits match what was built (RQ3). In one sentence: from above, we can see new buildings; new floors have to be found from the street.

- **RQ1 (Model).** Can LiDAR teach a foundation model to detect new construction and added storeys from yearly aerial imagery, in ZIP codes and a year it has never seen? At what spatial scale is it reliable enough to fill the years (r ≥ 0.8 at 250 m)?
- **RQ2 (Urban).** How much, when and where did Philadelphia densify between 2018 and 2025? How does this relate to rail transit and to neighbourhood income change?
- **RQ3 (Governance).** What share of permits turns into visible construction, and after how long? What share of visible change has no matching permit?

## Data

Code and data are kept apart: the data lives outside the repository (`~/data/musa6950-final/` on WSL), `data` in the repository is only a symbolic link to it and is not committed; the sources and download dates of the data behind the committed results are in `data_sources/`. After `data/` was deleted by accident on 2026-11-07, the public layers were downloaded again with `scripts/fetch_base.py` and `scripts/stage7_data.py`; LiDAR rasters, LiDAR change labels and imagery tiles were not rebuilt (see `data_sources/README.md`). `data/raw/` is raw data and is not modified: 2016 and 2025 parcels, 2017 building footprints, boundaries, L&I permits, the imagery tile cache (`data/raw/ortho/tiles/`, 9 years), and the raw LiDAR files that were on disk before streaming (`keep_raw_files.txt`). Citywide LiDAR is streamed: download → cut to the citywide 0.5 m grid (`data/interim/citywide_tiles/`) → delete the raw file. Rasters mosaicked per ZIP are in `data/interim/citywide_rasters/`, per-lot change labels in `data/interim/lidar_change_city/`.

Public data added in stage 7: `data/raw/li/permits_studyarea_2014_2025.csv` (permits, Carto), `data/raw/acs/` (ACS summary files, no API key needed), `data/raw/boundaries/tl_2020_42_bg.zip`, `data/raw/transit/gtfs_public.zip` (SEPTA); sources are noted in each folder's `SOURCE*.txt`. Stage 6 per-unit results are in `data/interim/stage6_units.parquet` and `data/interim/stage6_scores/`.

**Cloud server** (AutoDL, RTX 4080 SUPER 32 GB): shut down after stage 6 on 2026-10-28 and no longer needed. The final checkpoints (`unitcls_m4s_s{0,1}{,_grew}.pt`, with `SHA256SUMS_m4`) and the DINOv3 SAT backbone weights are back in `data/interim/models/` (2026-11-07, checksums verified), with another copy in the author's cloud drive. Website assets `web/public/data/` are not in git; when needed they are pulled back from the live site with `web/pipeline/pull_live_data.py`.

Files with street addresses (`review*.html`) stay local.
